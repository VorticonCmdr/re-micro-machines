Logo intro (a real animation -- click to skip; no key does) → code card (arrows move, ENTER accepts, twice) → GAME OPTIONS (F1/F2 control device, F3 sound, F4 smoothness including AUTO, F5 redefine keys, F6 credits; ENTER plays, ESC quits to DOS) → menu → character select → Challenge/Head-to-Head tournament (pick your 3 Challenge opponents yourself right after the qualifier; a real tournament board screen shows between Challenge races) → champion (M3.9). Arrows navigate menus, Space/Enter confirms (the race-intro and "IS OUT!!" screens: any key, on release, or ~10 s); during a race, arrows steer/throttle and S fires (KEYS2, or SETTINGS.DAT's own binding), SPACE pauses, and ESC quits the race back to the title screen. Head-to-Head vs CPU asks who you race; it has no results table and no screen after a won race (docs/engine.md §9an). Two-human head-to-head is not implemented (docs/engine.md §9k). A lap counts at the start grid (the bath rack in the qualifier; tick "Lap line (dev)" to mark it); finish in the top two, and in the qualifier completing a lap while ahead of everyone on laps wins it outright.
