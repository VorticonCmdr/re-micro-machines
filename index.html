ROUND21 (POWERBOATS, one player) — M3.6, no menus/two-player yet (PLAN-ENGINE.md). Controls: arrows to steer/throttle, S to fire, SPACE to pause, R to restart once the race is over. Fire is only enabled in TANKS (round 7) in the real game — this page always boots ROUND21 (round 2), so check "Projectiles (dev)" above to fire here anyway. As in the original, holding S on the keyboard freezes your car's steering and speed while it is held, even where nothing fires (docs/engine.md §9an). The lap counts where the left channel passes under the bath rack, right where you start (the original paints no line there; tick "Lap line (dev)" to mark it). The HUD's top digit is laps left: it jumps to 4 as the race starts (you start just behind the line) and counts down each time you cross it. If the game files aren't found at /game (a production build), open your own copy with "Open game folder…" or drop it below.
