Pick a Round and Race above to playtest any of the 29 tracks (POWERBOATS/ROUND21, one player — M3.6, no menus/two-player yet, PLAN-ENGINE.md). Controls: arrows to steer/throttle, S to fire, SPACE to pause, R to restart once the race is over. Fire is only enabled in TANKS (round 7) in the real game — pick round 7 above for real projectiles, or open any other round with ?dev and check "Projectiles (dev)" to fire there anyway. As in the original, holding S on the keyboard freezes your car's steering and speed while it is held, even where nothing fires (docs/engine.md §9an). The original paints no lap line of its own; with ?dev, "Lap line (dev)" marks the progress wrap at the start grid. The HUD's top digit is laps left: it jumps to 4 as the race starts (you start just behind the line) and counts down each time you cross it. If the game files aren't found at /game (a production build), open your own copy with "Open game folder…" or drop it below.
